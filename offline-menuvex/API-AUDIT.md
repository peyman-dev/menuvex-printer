## Desktop Capability Dispositions (`desktop/contracts/desktop-capabilities.json`)

The standalone Coffish Desktop application (`desktop/`) implements all core café operational domains locally on the desktop machine (`POST /local/*` and embedded `/api/v1/*`) while also maintaining full compatibility with the Sync v2 protocol when an optional remote server is configured:

- **POS & Order Management (`/api/v1/pos/*`, `/api/v1/orders/*`)**: Local POS checkout, table occupancy tracking, customer loyalty points, kitchen/bar order state transitions, automatic recipe (BOM) stock deduction on order completion, and 80mm Iranian thermal receipt printing.
- **Menu, Categories, Products & Banners (`/api/v1/categories/*`, `/api/v1/products/*`, `/api/v1/banners/*`)**: Direct local CRUD + structured optimistic-version editor.
- **Inventory, Ingredients, Stock Movements, Waste & Recipes (`/api/v1/inventory/*`)**: Local stock ledger (`purchase`, `consumption`, `waste`, `opening_balance`, `adjustment`), recipe BOM definitions, reorder point alerts, and counted-stock adjustments.
- **Floor Tables & Reservations (`/api/v1/tables/*`)**: Visual table occupancy management and guest reservation scheduling.
- **Customer Club & Debtors Ledger (`/api/v1/customers/*`, `/api/v1/debtors/*`)**: Customer profiles, loyalty tiers, credit ledger (`نسیه`), and partial/full debt settlement.
- **Finance, Expenses, Staff, Tasks & Reports (`/api/v1/expenses/*`, `/api/v1/staff/*`, `/api/v1/tasks/*`, `/api/v1/reports/*`)**: Expense tracking, staff shifts, daily barista checklists, and P&L financial analytics.
